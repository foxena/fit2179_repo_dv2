# Hand-drawn sketch checklist

The FIT2179 rubric says the sketch receives 0 if created with digital tools, so draw this by hand.

Recommended five sections:

1. Hero + story question
   - Title
   - Intro paragraph
   - Privacy note
   - Four KPI boxes: runs, distance, hours, elevation
   - Distance ladder showing 5 km, 10 km, half marathon, marathon, 50 km, the 50.15 km training run, 100 km and 161 km

2. Australian participation context
   - Large state/territory map
   - Ranked top activities chart
   - Age or organised-participation chart

3. Australian ultra-running benchmark
   - Ultra finishers by year
   - 50 km speed distribution
   - 50 km finish-time distribution
   - Distance summary bubble chart

4. Training build-up
   - Monthly layered chart
   - Weekly line/area chart
   - Calendar heatmap
   - Distance vs pace scatterplot
   - Heart rate/elevation chart

5. Custom insight and conclusion
   - Effort matrix bubble chart
   - Two short conclusion text boxes
   - Sources/footer

Take a clear photo or scan, export as PDF, upload it to GitHub, and paste the GitHub PDF URL into the assignment template.
